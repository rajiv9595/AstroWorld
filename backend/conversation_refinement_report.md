## 1. Defects Fixed & Root Cause Analysis

### DEF-01: Raw RAG Inline Citations in Conversational Prose
- **Severity**: P1
- **Affected Scenarios**: B1, B2, B3, C1, C3, C4, D2, D4
- **Symptom**: Verbatim classical sloka references (e.g., `According to BPHS Ch. 6, Sl. 26–28, D10 Dashamsha interpretation rule...`) were being dumped directly into normal conversational prose.
- **Root Cause**: The RAG retrieval records were being propagated without semantic translation from the candidate claim generator into the approved claim set, and the evidence selector in normal mode did not normalize citation headers into natural Jyotish principles.
- **Fix**: 
  - Maintained complete internal provenance metadata (`sourceId`, `ruleId`, `classicalContextSummary`).
  - Added semantic principle translation in `evidenceSelector.ts` (`normalizeClaimText`), converting raw sloka headers into natural Jyotish principles (`In classical Jyotish, ...`, `In classical Gochara principles, ...`) in normal user mode while preserving full citations in technical/source mode.

---

### DEF-02: Golden Jupiter Case Clause-Splicing
- **Severity**: P1
- **Affected Scenario**: C5 (`C5_JUPITER_PROMOTION_GOLDEN`)
- **Symptom**: Fragmented, malformed sentences such as `"Jupiter in D10 (Leo (House 4))., while According to Phaladeepika..."`
- **Root Cause**: In `geminiNarrator.ts`, deterministic paragraph composition concatenated raw fact strings containing trailing periods with `, while `, resulting in punctuation collisions (`)., while `) and ungrounded citation splices.
- **Fix**: Rebuilt the paragraph composition structure to format complete semantic claims cleanly:
  - Paragraph 1: Direct transit support & promotion momentum statement.
  - Paragraph 2: Natal Moon activation + D10 executive capacity + active Vimshottari Dasha confluence.
  - Paragraph 3: Classical qualification regarding conscious discipline and preparation.
  - Paragraph 4: Primary timing window bounded by verified peak confluence.

---

### DEF-03: A5 D10 Lagna Mismatch
- **Severity**: P0 / P1
- **Affected Scenario**: A5 (`A5_D10_LAGNA`)
- **Symptom**: User asked for D10 Lagna; the system returned `Sun in D10 (position: Libra 06° 58' 01")`.
- **Root Cause**: In `toolOrchestrator.ts`, the `get_divisional_chart` handler iterated over `data.planets` but omitted `data.ascendantSign` when constructing `FactItem` objects. Consequently, no D10 Lagna fact existed in the `EvidencePacket`, forcing `evidenceSelector` to pick the highest-ranked planetary placement instead.
- **Fix**:
  - Updated `toolOrchestrator.ts` to extract `data.ascendantSign` for all divisional charts (`D9`, `D10`, etc.) and create canonical Lagna facts (`factor: 'Lagna'`, `vargaSign: data.ascendantSign`).
  - Updated `questionPlanner.ts` to route D10/D9 Lagna queries into divisional chart tool plans.
  - Enhanced `evidenceSelector.ts` to grant a +120 priority boost to Lagna facts when the user explicitly queries Ascendant/Lagna.

---

### DEF-04: Raw Database Coordinate Formatting
- **Severity**: P2
- **Affected Scenarios**: A1, A2, A3, B5
- **Symptom**: Output exposed database tuples and debug notation like `(position: Sagittarius 09° 41' 45")` or `(sign: Aquarius)` in normal simple fact answers.
- **Root Cause**: Raw claim strings generated by `claimGenerator.ts` were rendered directly without conversational formatting in simple-fact response branches.
- **Fix**:
  - Added natural human phrasing rules in `evidenceSelector.ts` and `geminiNarrator.ts`.
  - Normal mode formats planetary positions conversationally (e.g., `"Your Moon is in Sagittarius (at 9° 41')."`, `"Your Ascendant (Lagna) is in Aquarius."`, `"Venus is placed in Libra (Swakshetra) at 4° 51' in the 9th house"`). Exact coordinate strings remain accessible in technical mode.
